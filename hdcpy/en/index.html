The problem

An operation spread
across 10 tabs

Hard Copy grew fast: dozens of active clients, a post-sales team, editors delivering creatives every week and a finance team checking contracts.

All of it lived scattered across ClickUp, Frame.io, Notion, spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. Answering "has this client paid yet?" meant opening four different tools.

  • Onboarding forms got lost, with no way to know who had filled in what
  • Deliveries sent to clients before finance confirmed the contract was paid
  • No view of productivity per editor or per account manager
  • Onboarding new hires depended on someone explaining everything again
Results

What changed
in the operation

2h → 10 minPer interaction · −92%

The internal customer service SaaS cut the time spent on each customer interaction from 2 hours to 10 minutes.

3 days → 1hAI + human review

A system combining AI with human review cut production time for one of the company's products from 3 days to 1 hour.

Automatic reportsPipelines and performance

Pipeline tracking plus reports on team performance, stage-by-stage progress and product delivery, all generated automatically.

3 departmentsSales · Post-sales · Admin

Automations connecting ClickUp, DataCrazy and Google Calendar, so nobody copies data from one place to another by hand.

The solution

One module for
each part of the operation

01 · CLIENTS

Instant client search

Type a name or email and the system instantly shows every form that client has filled in. New submissions sync on their own.

  • Real-time search as you type
  • Automatic form syncing
02 · CUSTOMER SERVICE

Post-sales team report

How many clients each team member contacted, how many new clients came in and who still has pending items for the day. Filter by today, yesterday, 7 days or month, and export to CSV.

  • Key metrics for the period up top
  • Per-person table with last activity
  • One-click spreadsheet export
03 · PROJECT MANAGEMENT

ClickUp summed up in charts

Nexus reads the ClickUp API and shows what changed over the last 7 days and how many tasks each person moved today, with a PDF ready for the meeting.

  • Direct ClickUp API integration
  • Daily chart and per-person ranking
  • PDF report generated on the spot
04 · DELIVERIES

The editor uploads, the system organizes

The editor enters the client code and drops in the videos. Nexus files everything in the right Frame.io project and notifies finance to check payment before anything is released to the client.

  • Direct upload to Frame.io via API
  • Final videos kept apart from editable files
  • Field validation before delivery
05 · FINANCE

No delivery goes out without a payment check

Each delivery becomes a card showing the contract, what has been paid and what is still owed. The system cross-checks the data and flags overdue installments or missing contracts on its own.

  • Automatic status: paid up, payment due, not identified
  • Anything left unchecked turns red the next day
  • Productivity per editor: ads, requests, revisions and clients
06 · KNOWLEDGE BASE

Training that doesn't depend on anyone

Template messages for every stage of client service, with placeholders you just swap for the client's details and a greeting that adjusts to the time of day. Plus the post-sales daily routine, step by step.

  • Ready-made messages organized by project stage
  • Daily routine with the "why it matters" for each block
07 · USERS

Everyone sees only what they need

Individual logins for every team member and per-screen permissions. Editors only see the delivery area, customer service sees clients and processes, admins see everything.

  • Granular per-screen permissions
  • Last-access log for every user
